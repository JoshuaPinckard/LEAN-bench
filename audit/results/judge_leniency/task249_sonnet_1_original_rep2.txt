The code uses SMA(9), SMA(26), and SMA(52) as Tenkan/Kijun/Senkou analogues, plus `CrossOver`, exactly as specified.

The main logic is fully implemented: long entry on upward crossover with price above both SMAs and bullish cloud condition, short entry on downward crossover with price below both SMAs and bearish cloud condition, and exits on reverse cross or price breaching the cloud boundary.

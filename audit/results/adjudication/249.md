## Why the spec pins behavior (quoted anchors)

- Entry trigger pinned: "Enter long when SMA(9) crosses SMA(26) from below to above at the bar
  close, with the closing price above both averages (SMA(26) and SMA(52)) and SMA(26) > SMA(52)"
  — direction, filter conditions, and confirmation timing are all explicit, and the mirror-image
  short entry is spelled out rather than left to inference.
- Exit pinned, both legs: "Exit long on the reverse cross ... or when the price closes below the
  lower of SMA(26) and SMA(52)" (and the mirrored short exit). The "lower of"/"higher of"
  min/max construction removes the usual which-average ambiguity.
- Indicator params pinned: "Indicators: SMA(9), SMA(26), SMA(52), CrossOver" — periods numeric,
  and CrossOver is named, matching backtrader's `bt.indicators.CrossOver` sign convention.
- No optional risk layers: "Stop-loss and take-profit are not used."
- Sizing stated: "Position size: 100% of available capital per trade" (see residual note below).
- Timing: "All signals are confirmed at the bar close" + D4a default next-bar-open fill.

## Per-ref analysis (genuineness of convergence)

The three codes are independently written (different class names, param structuring,
notify_order styles) and each maps the spec's rules directly rather than sharing a shortcut:

- **ref0 (opus, success, 13 trades):** `CrossOver(sma9, sma26)`; long entry
  `cross > 0 and close > sma26 and close > sma52 and sma26 > sma52`, mirrored short; exits
  `cross < 0 or close < min(sma26, sma52)` / `cross > 0 or close > max(...)`. Sizing
  `int(cash / (close * (1 + commission)))` — reserves the 0.001 fee. One-position, pending-order
  guard per D4a.
- **ref1 (opus, success, 13 trades):** identical entry/exit logic, structured independently;
  sizing via `order_target_percent(target=±1.0)` — a different backtrader mechanism entirely.
- **ref2 (sonnet, success, 13 trades):** identical entry/exit logic; sizing
  `int(broker.getcash() / close)` with no commission reserve; `notify_order` clears the guard
  only on terminal statuses (behaviorally equivalent to the early-return style).

Cross-model (opus x2 + sonnet) and cross-mechanism (three distinct sizing realizations)
agreement on the primary key is strong evidence the spec, not shared convention, pins the tape.

## Divergence 1: ref0 vs ref1 — exit evaluation semantics (`unpinned-exit`)

Spec: "**Take Profit:** Set the take profit target at a distance equal to the
height of the channel (the difference between the upper and lower bands at the
time of the breakout). For example, if the channel height is 5%, the take profit
will be 5% above the entry price." and "**Stop Loss:** Place a stop loss order
below the lower band of the channel". The spec pins the *levels* but never says
how they are monitored — resting orders, intrabar touch, or bar-close crossing —
nor what "the entry price" is (signal close vs actual fill), nor how far "below"
the lower band the stop sits (all three refs placed it exactly at the band).

- ref0 checks `low[0] <= stop` / `high[0] >= target` (intrabar touch) then exits
  with market `close()`; declared assumption: stop-first precedence on same-bar
  touch.
- ref1 checks `close[0] >= tp or close[0] <= sl` (bar-close only), also `close()`.

Verified on the frozen data: both refs computed identical levels for the first
trade (signal bar 2024-11-07 14:30, TP = 230.346, SL = 221.035). The intrabar
high first touched TP at 2024-11-22 17:30 → ref0 SELL fills 2024-11-22 18:30;
the close first crossed TP at 2024-11-25 14:30 → ref1 SELL fills
2024-11-25 15:30. Exactly the observed tape difference. Both are defensible
readings; no spec sentence adjudicates between them. D4a's canonical defaults
cover `self.close()` for exiting but not touch-vs-close trigger evaluation.

## Divergence 2: ref2 vs ref0/ref1 — falling-structure definition (`unpinned-entry-trigger`)

Spec: "Ensure the channel has been established with at least 3 consecutive lower
highs and lower lows to confirm the falling structure before considering a
breakout." The spec defines neither what a "high"/"low" is (per-bar extremes vs
swing pivots), nor a lookback window, nor a pivot definition.

- ref0/ref1: strictly consecutive **bar-wise** lower highs AND lower lows on the
  4 bars immediately preceding the breakout bar (`high[-1]<high[-2]<high[-3]<high[-4]`,
  same for lows).
- ref2: 1-bar local-extremum **swing pivots** inside a 20-bar window; any 3
  consecutive decreasing swing highs and any 3 decreasing swing lows qualify.

This is why ref2's first entry (2024-03-08 15:30) predates ref0/ref1's
(2024-11-07 15:30) by eight months. Both readings are faithful; "lower highs and
lower lows" is standard chartist language that legitimately refers to swing
pivots, and equally legitimately to consecutive bar extremes. Related unpinned
entry text that happened *not* to bite here: the channel indicator itself is an
explicit either/or ("Use Bollinger Bands (BB) ... **Alternatively, use a Donchian
Channel**" and entry on "either the Bollinger Bands upper line or the Donchian
Channel high") — all three refs happened to pick BB(20,2); and the volume filter
"higher than the average volume of the last 10 periods" — ref0 averages the
*previous* 10 bars, ref1/ref2 use SMA(10) including the current bar (identical
entry sets on this data, so not tape-visible).

## Divergence 3: ref2 never exits — implementation bug (repair candidate)

Spec: "**Stop Loss:** Place a stop loss order below the lower band of the channel
(the Bollinger Bands lower line or the Donchian Channel low)." and the take-profit
sentence quoted above. ref2's *code* places bracket Stop/Limit sells in
`notify_order` — but matches orders by object identity (`order is self.order`).
Instrumented re-execution on the frozen cache shows this comparison is **always
False**: backtrader's broker delivers *cloned* order objects to `notify_order`,
so the Completed branch never runs, the SL/TP orders are never submitted, and
`self.order` is never reset. Consequences, verified against the data: after the
2024-03-08 entry (fill 171.78, SL 168.40, TP 174.55), the TP level was touched
2024-03-18 and the SL level 2024-04-02, yet no exit ever occurred — the position
is held unprotected to the end of data (+41.6%), and the stuck pending-order
guard suppresses every later entry. This contradicts the explicit exit sentences
behaviorally (no stop loss order is ever placed, no take profit is ever set live)
and violates the D4a canonical default "pending-order guard reset in
`notify_order`". → ref2 listed in repair_candidates.

**Repair is moot for classification:** a fixed ref2 would still enter on
2024-03-08 via its faithful swing-pivot reading (divergent from ref0/ref1 for
spec-level reasons), and ref0 vs ref1 diverge regardless.

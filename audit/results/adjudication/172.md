## Where and why the tapes differ

**1. Ref0 vs Ref1 — SELL one hour apart (20:30 vs 19:30): SL/TP evaluation granularity
(`unpinned-exit`, compounded by `timeframe-mismatch`).**
The spec pins the exit *levels* — "stop-loss 2% below the entry price; take-profit 6% above" —
but not the *trigger mechanics*: whether the levels are monitored intrabar on the raw 1h feed or
only on completed 4H bars, and via which order type. Ref0 evaluates SL/TP "only on completed 4H
bars, not intrabar on 1h bars" (its declared `exit-evaluation-granularity` assumption; code:
`_check_exit` runs from `_process_h4` using the 4H bar's low/high). Ref1 monitors "intrabar on
each 1h bar using the bar's low/high" (its `stop/take-profit mechanics` assumption; code: the
SL/TP block at the top of `next()` runs on every 1h bar). Both took the same trade (BUY filled
2025-12-05 16:30 at 10.93); ref1's 1h-granularity stop detected the 2% breach a bar earlier, so
it sold at 19:30 vs ref0's 20:30. Both are stop-loss exits (fills 10.69 / 10.655 vs stop
10.7114). This choice only exists because a strategy specified "on the 4-hour chart" is bound to
a 1h feed — the raw-1h-vs-synthetic-4H monitoring option is an artifact of the D2 binding.

**2. Ref2 vs Ref0/Ref1 — zero trades: divergence→breakout sequencing and volume-SMA window
(`unpinned-entry-trigger`).**
The spec says the divergence "forms" and "**then** a resistance breakout occurs" but gives no
validity window tying the two. Ref0 and ref1 arm a persistent latch when a divergence is
confirmed ("the flag persists until used" / "the latch is not otherwise time-expired") and enter
on the first later bar with breakout+volume. Ref2 instead evaluates "all three entry conditions …
simultaneously on each completed 4H bar", requiring the two most recent confirmed swing lows to
still form a divergence at the breakout bar (its declared `divergence and breakout sequencing`
assumption) — a far stricter conjunction. Ref2 also computes SMA(volume, 20) over the 20 bars
"preceding (not including) the current bar", while ref0/ref1 include the current bar; the spec's
"volume > SMA(volume, 20)" does not pin the window's inclusion of the signal bar. Under these
readings no bar in Nov–Dec 2025 satisfies all three conditions at once, hence the empty tape.
Both readings are defensible; neither contradicts any spec sentence.

**3. Pivot definition (`unpinned-entry-trigger`, latent).**
"The previous local low" / "local high" has no pivot/swing definition in the spec. Ref0 and ref2
use symmetric 2-bar fractals; ref1 uses 1-bar pivots ("the minimal 1-bar pivot is the simplest
literal reading"). On this data the entry bar happened to coincide for ref0/ref1, but the
detector choice is a live degree of freedom that ref2's simultaneous-evaluation reading interacts
with.

**Points of convergence (not divergence sources):** all three aggregate the 1h feed into
synthetic 4H bars by naive 4-consecutive-bar grouping (the shared `infeasible` assumption forced
by the 4H-prose/1h-binding mismatch); all three exclude the current bar from the 20-bar breakout
high ("a close above the high of the last 20 bars" — a bar cannot close above its own high); all
three use `broker.getvalue()` as "capital" and the signal bar's 4H close as the "entry price" in
the sizing formula; all three apply the Nov–Dec 2025 filter to entries only.

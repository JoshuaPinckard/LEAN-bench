## Where the tapes diverge and which spec gap explains each difference

### 1. ref0 vs ref1 — exit-only divergence → `unpinned-exit`

The two opus refs enter on identical timestamps (2025-08-13T14:00, 2025-08-27T19:00, 2025-09-08T07:00) but exit at different times on trades 1 and 3 (ref1: 2025-08-13T15:00 and 2025-09-08T11:00; ref0: 2025-08-14T13:00 and 2025-09-08T14:00). Cause: the alternative exit. The spec says only:

> "*(Alternative exit)* If signs of a reversal downward appear (for example, formation of a bearish candlestick pattern on increased volume)."

"For example" pins nothing. ref0 encoded a **bearish engulfing** candle + `volume[0] > volume[-1]`; ref1 encoded **any bearish candle** (`close < open`) + `volume[0] > volume[-1]`. ref1's looser pattern fires the bar after entry on trades 1 and 3, producing earlier SELLs. Both are faithful readings of an unnamed "bearish candlestick pattern"; neither can be repaired against quoted text.

### 2. ref2 vs ref0/ref1 — entry divergence → `unpinned-entry-trigger`, `unpinned-indicator-params`

ref2 produces one trade in March 2026 instead of three in Aug–Sep 2025. Three unpinned entry choices compound:

- **Volume baseline.** Spec: "breaks above the resistance level 6496.25 (the last high) **with increased volume**" and "confirmed by **rising trading volume** at the moment of the breakout" — no lookback, no baseline. ref0/ref1: `volume[0] > volume[-1]` (bar-over-bar). ref2: `volume[0] > SMA(volume, 20)` — and the 20-period window is itself an invented parameter (`unpinned-indicator-params`; ref2's assumption: "The spec requires above-average volume at the breakout but does not specify the lookback window"). This filter alone selects different breakout bars.
- **Recovery definition.** Spec: "A **pullback from the key support at 6430.75 has occurred, and the price has started to recover**" — recovery is undefined. ref0/ref1: `close[0] > close[-1]`; ref2: `close[0] > 6430.75`. Also different flag-reset conventions (ref0/ref1 reset on entry; ref2 resets on sell completion).
- **Optional trend filter.** Spec: "Trend indicator **(optional for confirmation)**: 200-period simple moving average (SMA) on the daily timeframe". ref0 and ref1 include it as a binding gate (reconstructing daily closes from the 1h feed, requiring a full 200-day warm-up); ref2 omits it entirely. "Optional" makes both readings faithful and makes the trace non-unique by construction.

### 3. Exit trigger basis → `unpinned-exit`

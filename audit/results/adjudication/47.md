## Where the tapes diverge and which unpinned spec text each difference maps to

Both refs share the identical high-level skeleton (internal 4h aggregation on `hour // 4`
UTC buckets from the single 5m feed; long-only single position; size = 1% of value /
(entry − stop) with an affordability cap; stop at the OB body's opposite side; RR = 3.333
from the $300→$1000 example; partial close of half at 50% of the TP distance, remainder at
100%; force-close after 48 5m bars; weekend block on new entries only). The divergence is
entirely in how the entry-detection machinery was operationalized:

1. **Order Block "strong candle" threshold** — spec: *"This is the body of the last strong
   candle before a sharp price move."* No definition of "strong" or "sharp."
   - ref0: any bearish 5m candle immediately followed by a bullish candle with a *larger
     body*, scanned over 20 bars (`body_up > body_down`).
   - ref1: last bearish 5m candle whose body ≥ **1.5× the 20-bar average body**, and the OB
     must lie within 48 bars of the breakout.
   ref1's threshold + recency filter makes qualifying OBs far rarer — the main driver of
   25 fills vs 2. Class: **unpinned-indicator-params**.

2. **Breakout trigger** — spec: *"Open a long position (buy) on an upward breakout of this
   Order Block."* Level vs cross is unpinned.
   - ref0: `close[0] > ob_top` on any bar while the setup is armed (level test).
   - ref1: `prev_close <= ob_top and close > ob_top` (strict cross).
   Class: **unpinned-entry-trigger**.

3. **FVG "price enters the zone" confirmation granularity** — spec: *"On the 4-hour
   timeframe, price enters the Fair Value Gap (FVG) zone"* and *"The strategy looks for
   cases when price returns to this zone."* The bench binds a **single 5m feed**, so the 4h
   layer is reconstructed in-strategy and the spec never says whether the condition is
   confirmed at 4h-candle close or intra-candle, nor how many FVGs stay live.
   - ref0: keeps **all** bullish FVGs; entry-into-zone confirmed only when a **later
     completed 4h candle** trades into one (`fvg['idx'] < n - 1`).
   - ref1: keeps only the **most recent** bullish FVG; confirmed when any **5m bar's range**
     touches it (which can fire almost immediately after formation).
   Classes: **unpinned-entry-trigger**, **timeframe-mismatch** (dual 4h+5m prose vs a
   single bound 5m feed forces this reconstruction; the confirmation-timing choice is a
   direct artifact of it).

4. **MSS break test** — spec: *"It occurs when price breaks the previous extreme (a high
   for a bearish shift, a low for a bullish shift)."* "Breaks" is unpinned.
   - ref0: 4h **low** pierces the last confirmed swing low.
   - ref1: 4h **close** below the last confirmed swing low.
   Class: **unpinned-entry-trigger**. (Both faithfully implemented the spec's own
   counter-intuitive rule that a *bullish* shift is a break of the previous *low*, per its
   condition-3 example.)

5. **Setup validity window** — spec: conditions may form *"Simultaneously or
   sequentially"* with **no stated window**. Both refs independently invented 24h
   (ref0: 6 completed 4h candles; ref1: 288 5m bars) — a coincidental match in nominal
   value measured at different granularities; any other choice would have been equally
   faithful. Class: **unpinned-entry-trigger**.

Where the spec *did* pin behavior (1% risk sizing, 50%/100% partial take-profit structure,
RR from the 1:3.33 example, stop on the OB's opposite side once that branch of the
either/or is taken, 4h max hold, weekend inactivity), the two refs converged exactly —
evidence that the divergence is spec-level, not sloppiness.
